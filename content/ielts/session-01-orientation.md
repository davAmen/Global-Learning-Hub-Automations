## Session Content

### 1. IELTS Test Format Overview

| Skill | Duration | Questions | Academic vs General |
|-------|----------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Listening** | 30 min + 10 min transfer | 40 | Same for both |
| **Reading** | 60 minutes | 40 | Different passages |
| **Writing** | 60 minutes | 2 tasks | Task 1 differs |
| **Speaking** | 11-14 minutes | 3 parts | Same for both |

**Total test time:** 2 hours 45 minutes

### 2. Band Score Descriptors (Key Bands)

| Band | Level | Description |
|------|-------|-------------|
| 9 | Expert | Fully operational command |
| 8 | Very Good | Occasional inaccuracies |
| 7 | Good | Operational with occasional errors |
| 6 | Competent | Generally effective despite errors |
| 5 | Modest | Partial command, frequent errors |
| 4 | Limited | Basic competence, frequent breakdowns |

**Target for most students:** Band 6.5-7.5

### 3. Course Expectations

- **Attendance:** 14 sessions mandatory (recordings available for 48h)
- **Homework:** 1-2 hours daily (submitted via WhatsApp)
- **Participation:** Active in WhatsApp group, peer feedback
- **Mock tests:** 2 full mocks (mid-course + final)
- **Certificate:** Awarded for ≥80% attendance + completed mock tests

## Diagnostic Mini-Test

**Instructions:** Complete both sections in 20 minutes total. No dictionaries.

### Listening (10 minutes)
*Audio will be played once. Write answers on answer sheet.*

**Section 1:** Conversation between student and accommodation officer
- Questions 1-5: Form completion (name, dates, preferences)
- Questions 6-10: Multiple choice

### Reading (10 minutes)
*Read the passage and answer questions 1-7.*

**Passage:** "The History of Coffee" (approx. 300 words)
- Questions 1-3: True/False/Not Given
- Questions 4-7: Matching headings
